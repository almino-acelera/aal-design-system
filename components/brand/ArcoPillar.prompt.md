The signature block for the **Arquitetura ARCO™**. Always shown as a set of four, in order, never three or five.

```jsx
<ArcoPillar letter="A" index={1} name="Análise Estratégica da Lacuna™" description="Diagnosticar a realidade atual, o estado desejado e o gap com impacto." />
```

Pass the prop as `"A" | "R" | "C" | "O"`; the component renders the ornate capital only for **A** and lowercase glyphs for **r · c · o** — read across the four pillars it spells *Arco*. The letter is Cinzel Decorative in gold at ~40px — the only place display type appears at small sizes. Descriptions are one sentence, present tense, second person or infinitive.
