Opens every section. The four-part rhythm — gold eyebrow, Cinzel title, gold hairline, light lead — is the brand's most recognizable layout signature; don't reorder it.

```jsx
<SectionHeading eyebrow="Arquitetura ARCO™" title="Diagnóstico em decisão, decisão em compromisso"
  lead="Quatro movimentos com postura consultiva transversal." align="center" />
```

Use `onDark` in olive sections. Titles are sentence case; the eyebrow is the only uppercase element.
