Status marker in uppercase micro type — module progress, turma status, lote de preço.

```jsx
<Badge tone="gold" dot>Turma aberta</Badge>
<Badge tone="success">Concluído</Badge>
```

Tones: `olive | gold | success | warning | danger | neutral | onDark`. `solid` fills the badge — use for at most one emphasized status per view. Keep labels to one or two words; badges are never sentences.
