Use `Button` for every action on Aline Loiola surfaces — uppercase Montserrat label with wide tracking, 4px radius, never pill-shaped.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" size="lg" href="#aplicar">Quero minha vaga</Button>
<Button variant="outline">Ver a Arquitetura ARCO™</Button>
```

Variants: `primary` (olive, the default), `secondary` (gold fill — use on olive/photo backgrounds or as the second-strongest action), `outline`, `ghost` (text-level), `accent` (orange — at most one per screen, for scarcity/urgency CTAs), `onDark` (hairline gold border for dark sections). Sizes `sm | md | lg`; `lg` is the hero CTA size. `iconLeft`/`iconRight` take a 16px Lucide node. `fullWidth` for mobile stacks and forms.
